# Record-to-Report (R2R) Process

## 📘 Overview

Record-to-Report (R2R) is the end-to-end finance process used to record business transactions, perform reconciliations and closing activities, and prepare financial reports.

For Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd., R2R brings together the company's financial transactions into accurate and structured financial reporting.

---

## 🔄 R2R Process Flow

**Business Transactions → G/L Posting → Reconciliation → Period-End Closing → Financial Reporting**

The process ensures that financial information is recorded accurately and reported within the required period.

---

## 1. Recording Business Transactions

Business transactions from different processes are recorded in SAP.

Examples include:

- Vendor invoices
- Customer invoices
- Vendor payments
- Customer receipts
- Asset transactions
- Bank transactions
- Expense postings
- Inventory-related accounting transactions

---

## 2. General Ledger Posting

Financial transactions are posted to appropriate G/L accounts.

Examples:

**Vendor Invoice**

Debit: Expense / Inventory  
Credit: Accounts Payable

**Customer Invoice**

Debit: Accounts Receivable  
Credit: Sales Revenue

**Vendor Payment**

Debit: Accounts Payable  
Credit: Bank

---

## 3. Account Reconciliation

Reconciliation is performed to verify that financial records are accurate and consistent.

Common reconciliation activities include:

- Bank reconciliation
- Vendor reconciliation
- Customer reconciliation
- G/L account reconciliation
- Subledger-to-G/L reconciliation
- GR/IR reconciliation

---

## 4. Period-End Closing

At the end of an accounting period, Finance performs various closing activities.

Typical activities may include:

- Review of open items
- Bank reconciliation
- Vendor and customer reconciliation
- Depreciation posting
- Accruals and provisions
- Foreign currency valuation, where applicable
- Review of G/L balances
- Closing of accounting periods

The exact closing activities depend on the company's processes and configuration.

---

## 5. Financial Reporting

After the financial records are reviewed and closing activities are completed, financial reports can be prepared.

Examples include:

- Balance Sheet
- Profit and Loss Statement
- Trial Balance
- G/L account balances
- Vendor balances
- Customer balances
- Asset reports
