## 🎯 Project Objective

The objective of this project is to develop practical understanding of SAP FICO and S/4HANA Finance by simulating finance-related business processes for a fictional manufacturing company.

The project focuses on understanding how business transactions are recorded, processed, analyzed and connected across different SAP Finance areas.

- Record-to-Report (R2R)
- Month-End Closing

---

## 🔄 End-to-End Process Understanding

### Procure-to-Pay

**Purchase → Goods Receipt → Invoice Receipt → Vendor Payment**

The P2P process demonstrates how procurement activities are connected with vendor accounting and payment processing.

---

### Order-to-Cash

**Sales Order → Delivery → Billing → Customer Payment**

The O2C process demonstrates how sales activities flow into customer receivables and collection.

---

### Record-to-Report

**Business Transaction → G/L Posting → Reconciliation → Period-End Closing → Financial Reporting**

The R2R process demonstrates how individual financial transactions contribute to period-end accounting and reporting.
