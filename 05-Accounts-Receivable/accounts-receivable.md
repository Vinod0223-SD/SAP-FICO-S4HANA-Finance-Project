# Accounts Receivable

## 📘 Overview

Accounts Receivable (AR) manages financial transactions and outstanding amounts related to customers.

For Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd., AR activities include customer master data, customer invoicing, incoming payments and customer account clearing.

## 🔄 Order-to-Cash Finance Flow

**Sales Order → Delivery → Billing → Customer Invoice → Customer Payment**

The Accounts Receivable process mainly handles the financial impact of customer billing and payment.

## 🧾 Customer Invoice Processing

When a customer is billed for goods or services, the appropriate customer receivable and revenue accounts are updated.

### Example

The company sells finished goods worth INR 75,000 on credit.

Accounting impact:

**Debit:** Accounts Receivable – INR 75,000  
**Credit:** Sales Revenue – INR 75,000

This creates an outstanding receivable from the customer.

---

## 💰 Incoming Customer Payment

When the customer makes the payment, the incoming payment is recorded against the outstanding receivable.

Example:

**Debit:** Bank – INR 75,000  
**Credit:** Accounts Receivable – INR 75,000

The customer open item is then cleared against the payment.

---

## 🔁 Customer Clearing

Customer clearing matches an outstanding customer invoice with its corresponding payment or credit transaction.

Example:

**Customer Invoice → Open Item → Incoming Payment → Clearing**
