# Bank Accounting

## 📘 Overview

Bank Accounting manages the company's bank-related financial transactions and helps maintain accurate records of cash and bank balances.

For Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd., Bank Accounting covers incoming payments, outgoing payments, bank transactions and bank reconciliation.

## 🔄 Common Bank Accounting Flows

### Incoming Payment

**Customer → Bank → Accounts Receivable → Customer Clearing**

### Outgoing Payment

**Vendor → Accounts Payable → Bank → Vendor Clearing**

### Bank Reconciliation

**Bank Statement → Compare with SAP Records → Identify Differences → Reconcile**

---

## 1. Incoming Customer Payment

When a customer pays an outstanding invoice, the payment is recorded in the company's bank account.

Example:

Customer pays INR 75,000.

**Debit:** Bank – INR 75,000  
**Credit:** Accounts Receivable – INR 75,000

The customer open item can then be cleared.

---

## 2. Outgoing Vendor Payment

When the company pays a vendor, the payment is recorded against the vendor liability.

Example:

Vendor payment of INR 50,000.

**Debit:** Accounts Payable – INR 50,000  
**Credit:** Bank – INR 50,000

The vendor open item can then be cleared.

---

## 3. Bank Charges

Banks may charge fees for services such as transactions or account maintenance.

Example:

Bank charges of INR 1,000.

**Debit:** Bank Charges Expense – INR 1,000  
**Credit:** Bank – INR 1,000

---

## 4. Bank Reconciliation

Bank reconciliation compares the company's SAP bank records with the bank statement.

The purpose is to identify differences such as:

- Unrecorded bank charges
- Outstanding payments
- Outstanding deposits
- Bank interest
- Timing differences
- Incorrect or missing entries

After identifying differences, appropriate accounting adjustments can be recorded.

---

## 5. Bank Statement Processing

Bank statements provide details of transactions processed through the company's bank account.

The Finance team reviews bank statement transactions and matches them with corresponding SAP transactions.

This supports accurate cash management and reconciliation.

---

## 🔗 Integration with SAP Finance

Bank Accounting is connected with several Finance processes:

**Accounts Receivable → Bank**

Customer payments increase the company's bank balance.

**Accounts Payable → Bank**

Vendor payments reduce the company's bank balance.

**General Ledger → Bank**

Bank-related transactions are reflected in the appropriate G/L accounts.
