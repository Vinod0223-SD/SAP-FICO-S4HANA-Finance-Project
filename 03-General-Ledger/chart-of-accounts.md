# Chart of Accounts

## 📘 Overview

The Chart of Accounts (CoA) is a structured list of G/L accounts used by Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd. to record and classify financial transactions.

For this practice project, a fictional operating Chart of Accounts is used to understand how G/L accounts are organized and used in SAP S/4HANA Finance.

## 🎯 Purpose of the Chart of Accounts

The Chart of Accounts is used to:

- Organize G/L accounts systematically
- Record financial transactions
- Classify assets, liabilities, income and expenses
- Support financial reporting
- Enable consistent accounting across the company

---

## 📊 G/L Account Categories

The main categories used in this project are:

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| **Assets** | Resources owned or controlled by the company |
| **Liabilities** | Financial obligations of the company |
| **Equity** | Owner's interest in the company |
| **Revenue** | Income generated from business activities |
| **Expenses** | Costs incurred to operate the business |

## 🔄 Example Accounting Flow

A typical vendor invoice may involve:

**Expense / Inventory G/L → Accounts Payable → Vendor Payment → Bank**

A customer transaction may involve:

**Accounts Receivable → Sales Revenue → Customer Payment → Bank**

These transactions are recorded using appropriate G/L accounts.
