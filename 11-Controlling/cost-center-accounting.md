# Cost Center Accounting

## 📘 Overview

Cost Center Accounting is a component of SAP Controlling (CO) used to monitor, analyze and control costs incurred by different departments or areas of a company.

For Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd., cost centers are used to track departmental expenses and support internal management reporting.

## 🎯 Purpose of Cost Centers

Cost centers help the company to:

- Track departmental expenses
- Compare actual costs with planned costs
- Identify areas of high expenditure
- Support budgeting and planning
- Analyze cost performance
- Improve cost control

---

## 🧾 Example: Expense Posting

Suppose the Finance department incurs an electricity expense of INR 10,000.

The accounting entry may be:

**Debit:** Electricity Expense – INR 10,000  
**Cost Center:** CC2000 – Finance

**Credit:** Bank / Accounts Payable – INR 10,000

The expense is therefore assigned to the Finance cost center for internal reporting.

---

## 🔄 Cost Center Accounting Flow

**Business Expense → G/L Account → Cost Center → Cost Analysis → Management Reporting**

The G/L account records the financial impact, while the cost center identifies where the cost was incurred.

---

## 📊 Actual vs Planned Costs

Cost Center Accounting can be used to compare:

**Planned Cost ↔ Actual Cost → Variance Analysis**

Example:

| Cost Center | Planned Cost | Actual Cost | Variance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Finance | INR 100,000 | INR 95,000 | INR 5,000 Favorable |
| Production | INR 500,000 | INR 530,000 | INR 30,000 Unfavorable |
| HR | INR 150,000 | INR 145,000 | INR 5,000 Favorable |

Variance analysis helps management understand differences between planned and actual expenditure.

---

## 🔗 Integration with Financial Accounting

Cost Center Accounting is integrated with Financial Accounting.

Example:

**FI Expense Posting → Cost Center Assignment → CO Reporting**

When an expense is posted to a relevant G/L account with a cost center, the transaction can also be analyzed in Controlling.
