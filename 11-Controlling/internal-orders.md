# Internal Orders

## 📘 Overview

Internal Orders are Controlling objects used to plan, collect and monitor costs for specific activities, events or temporary projects within an organization.

For Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd., Internal Orders can be used to track costs that need separate monitoring from normal departmental expenses.

---

## 🎯 Purpose of Internal Orders

Internal Orders help the company to:

- Collect costs for specific activities
- Monitor project-related expenses
- Track event or campaign costs
- Compare planned and actual costs
- Support internal cost analysis
- Settle costs to appropriate receivers

---

## 🏢 Sample Internal Orders

| Internal Order | Description | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| IO1001 | Employee Training | Track training-related expenses |
| IO1002 | Factory Maintenance | Track special maintenance costs |
| IO1003 | Marketing Campaign | Track campaign expenses |
| IO1004 | IT Upgrade Project | Track IT project costs |

These Internal Orders are fictional and created for learning purposes.

---

## 🧾 Example: Marketing Campaign

Suppose the company spends INR 50,000 on a marketing campaign.

The expense can be posted with:

**G/L Account:** Marketing Expense  
**Amount:** INR 50,000  
**Internal Order:** IO1003 – Marketing Campaign

The Internal Order collects the cost so that management can monitor the total expenditure for the campaign.

---

## 🔄 Internal Order Process

**Business Expense → G/L Account → Internal Order → Cost Collection → Settlement / Analysis**

Internal Orders help identify the specific activity responsible for the cost.

---

## 📊 Planned vs Actual Cost

Internal Orders can be used to compare planned and actual costs.

Example:

| Internal Order | Planned Cost | Actual Cost | Variance |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| IO1001 | INR 80,000 | INR 75,000 | INR 5,000 Favorable |
| IO1002 | INR 120,000 | INR 135,000 | INR 15,000 Unfavorable |
| IO1003 | INR 100,000 | INR 90,000 | INR 10,000 Favorable |

Variance analysis helps management understand cost performance.

---

## 🔁 Settlement

At the appropriate stage, costs collected on an Internal Order may be settled to a relevant receiver.

Possible receivers can include:

- Cost Center
- Asset
- G/L Account
- Other Controlling objects

The actual settlement process depends on the business requirement and SAP configuration.

---

## 🔗 Integration with Financial Accounting

Internal Orders can receive costs from Financial Accounting postings.

Example:

**FI Expense Posting → Internal Order → CO Cost Analysis**

This allows Finance and Controlling teams to analyze costs from both financial and management accounting perspectives.
