# Company Overview

## 🏢 Company Profile

**Company Name:** Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd.

**Industry:** Manufacturing

**Business Type:** Private Manufacturing Company

**Project Type:** SAP FICO & S/4HANA Finance Practice Project

---

## 📋 Business Background

Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd. is a fictional manufacturing company created for this SAP Finance practice project.

The company manufactures and sells industrial components to business customers. It purchases raw materials from various vendors, processes them through its manufacturing operations, and sells finished products to customers.

The project simulates how SAP S/4HANA Finance can support the company's financial accounting and controlling activities.

---

## 🏭 Business Operations

The major business activities of the company include:

1. Procurement of raw materials
2. Receipt of purchased materials
3. Vendor invoice processing
4. Vendor payment processing
5. Manufacturing of finished goods
6. Sales of finished products
7. Customer billing
8. Customer payment collection
9. Asset acquisition and depreciation
10. Bank transactions
11. Cost center management
12. Month-end financial closing

---

## 💼 Finance Functions

The Finance department is responsible for:

- General Ledger Accounting
- Accounts Payable
- Accounts Receivable
- Asset Accounting
- Bank Accounting
- Cost Center Accounting
- Financial reporting
- Period-end closing

---

## 🔄 Key Business Processes

| Process | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **P2P** | Manage procurement and vendor-related financial activities |
| **O2C** | Manage sales, billing and customer collections |
| **R2R** | Manage accounting, reconciliation and financial reporting |
| **AP** | Manage vendor-related transactions |
| **AR** | Manage customer-related transactions |
| **AA** | Manage company assets and depreciation |
| **Bank Accounting** | Manage bank-related transactions |
| **CO** | Monitor and control business costs |

## 📌 Project Assumptions

For learning and simulation purposes, the following assumptions are made:

- The company operates in India.
- Financial transactions are recorded in INR.
- The company has vendors and customers.
- The company maintains bank accounts.
- The company owns fixed assets.
- The company uses cost centers to monitor expenses.
- SAP S/4HANA Finance concepts are used throughout the project.
