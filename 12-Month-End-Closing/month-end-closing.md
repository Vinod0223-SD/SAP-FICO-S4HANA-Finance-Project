# Month-End Closing

## 📘 Overview

Month-end closing is the process of reviewing, reconciling and finalizing financial transactions for an accounting period.

For Nikhil Manufacturing Pvt. Ltd., the month-end closing process helps ensure that financial records are complete and accurate before financial reporting.

---

## 🔄 Month-End Closing Flow

**Transaction Review → Reconciliation → Adjustments → Depreciation → Accruals → Account Review → Period Closing → Reporting**

The exact sequence and activities depend on the company's processes and SAP configuration.

---

## 1. Transaction Review

Finance reviews transactions recorded during the accounting period.

Typical checks include:

- G/L postings
- Vendor invoices
- Customer invoices
- Payments and receipts
- Asset transactions
- Bank transactions
- Expense postings

The objective is to identify missing, incorrect or unusual transactions.

---

## 2. Accounts Payable and Receivable Review

Finance reviews vendor and customer accounts.

Activities may include:

- Review vendor open items
- Review customer open items
- Identify overdue items
- Perform vendor reconciliation
- Perform customer reconciliation
- Review uncleared transactions

---

## 3. Bank Reconciliation

Bank records are compared with SAP records.

The Finance team investigates differences such as:

- Unrecorded bank charges
- Outstanding payments
- Outstanding deposits
- Bank interest
- Timing differences

Required adjustments are recorded where appropriate.

---

## 4. Asset Accounting

Depreciation is reviewed and posted for the accounting period.

Example:

**Debit:** Depreciation Expense  
**Credit:** Accumulated Depreciation

Finance also reviews asset balances and investigates unusual movements.

---

## 5. Accruals and Provisions

Expenses relating to the current accounting period may need to be recognized even when the related invoice has not yet been received.

Example:

A service worth INR 20,000 was received during the month, but the vendor invoice has not yet arrived.

A simplified accrual entry may be:

**Debit:** Relevant Expense – INR 20,000  
**Credit:** Accrued Expense / Provision – INR 20,000

The actual accounting treatment depends on company policy and configuration.

---

## 6. G/L Account Reconciliation

Important G/L accounts are reviewed and reconciled.

Examples include:

- Bank accounts
- Accounts Receivable
- Accounts Payable
- Inventory-related accounts
- Fixed asset accounts
- Accrued expense accounts

The objective is to ensure that balances are accurate and supported by appropriate records.

---

## 7. Cost Center Review

Controlling activities are reviewed at period end.

Finance and Controlling teams may analyze:

- Actual costs
- Planned costs
- Cost variances
- Internal Order balances
- Cost allocations

---

## 8. Period Closing

After required checks and adjustments are completed, the accounting period may be closed according to the company's period-end procedure.

This helps prevent unauthorized postings to a completed accounting period.
